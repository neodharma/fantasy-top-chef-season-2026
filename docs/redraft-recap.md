# Mid-Season Redraft Recap

After 8 episodes, 7 of 15 chefs were eliminated, leaving everyone with half-empty rosters. The mid-season redraft refreshed all 10 teams from the surviving 8-chef pool.

See [`how-the-redraft-works.md`](./how-the-redraft-works.md) for the full mechanic. Short version: each team optionally kept 1 chef, then ranked the remaining survivors. Pick order = reverse current standings (worst-first), snake. Per-chef cap raised to 4. Each team ends at 3 chefs total. Effective from episode 9.

## Notable outcomes

- **House Young** picked last (10th seed → 1st in reverse) and walked away with three of the most-picked chefs: Anthony (kept) + Laurence + Sherry.
- **Padma's Angels** chose not to keep and paid for it on the back end — their R2 and R3 picks were #5 and #6 on their list, since #2–#4 had all hit the cap by their R2 turn.
- **Risotto Roughnecks** got both top-2 wishes — Sherry (#1) and Oscar (#2) — added to a kept Rhoda.
- All 10 rosters are unique. Closest pairs share 2 chefs (e.g., Risotto Roughnecks and Best with Broth both have Rhoda + Sherry).

## What changes on the site

- Episodes 1–8 scoring stays with the original-draft owners (no retroactive change).
- Episodes 9+ scoring credits the new redraft rosters.
- Standings cards on `/standings` toggle between "original" and "redraft" views.
